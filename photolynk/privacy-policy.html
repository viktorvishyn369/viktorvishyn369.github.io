Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

StealthLynk LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the PhotoLynk application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

1. Service Overview

PhotoLynk is a photo and video backup application that operates in three distinct modes:

  • Local Mode: Your data is stored on a server you own and operate on your local network.
  • Remote Mode: Your data is stored on a server you own and operate, accessible over the internet.
  • StealthCloud Mode: Your data is encrypted on your device before upload and stored on StealthLynk-operated infrastructure.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local and Remote Modes

When using Local or Remote modes, all data remains on servers you own and control. StealthLynk LLC does not have access to, collect, or store your photos, videos, email address, password, device identifiers, or any file metadata.

You are solely responsible for the security, maintenance, and backup of your self-hosted server and all data stored thereon.

2.2 StealthCloud Mode

When using StealthCloud mode, we collect and process:

  • Account Information: Email address for authentication purposes (if you register with email), or a wallet-derived internal address (if you sign in with a Solana wallet)
  • Password: Stored only as a bcrypt hash — we never store or can read your plaintext password. Password reset codes are sent to your email address and expire after 15 minutes
  • Device Identifiers: Hardware device identifiers for security, session binding, and account recovery
  • Encrypted Data: Photos and videos encrypted on your device before transmission
  • Subscription Information: Payment and subscription status (processed by third-party payment processors)
  • Public Wallet Address: Your Solana wallet address, used to verify on-chain payments and link your account across StealthLynk apps. It is a public key — we never see or store private keys or seed phrases

Important: All media files and metadata are encrypted on your device using industry-standard encryption (XSalsa20-Poly1305) before transmission. StealthLynk LLC stores only encrypted data and does not possess the decryption keys. We cannot access, view, or decrypt your files.

2.3 In-App Purchase Information

When you make in-app purchases, we collect and process:

  • On-Chain Payment Records: The Solana transaction signature, paying wallet address, amount, and plan purchased — used to verify your payment on-chain
  • Account Balance: Credit balance and transaction history for photo certification services
  • Premium Status: Whether you have purchased the Premium tier ($49.99 USD one-time) and associated feature entitlements (1TB encrypted cloud storage for 1 year, 100 completely free certifications)
  • Verification Credit: Photo Verification Credit purchases ($15.00 USD consumable) that add to your account balance for photo certification services
  • Platform Information: The app (PhotoLynk or another StealthLynk app) where the purchase was made

Payments are made from your own Solana wallet and verified against public on-chain transaction data. We do not store or process payment card information.

Transaction records, including purchase amounts, dates, and certification service usage, are retained for accounting, tax compliance, and customer support purposes.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: Used only to scan QR codes for server pairing / local setup.
  • Photos/Videos: Used to back up your media, restore it, and scan for duplicates.
  • Network: Used to connect to your local server, remote server, or StealthCloud.

4. Data Encryption and Security

StealthCloud implements end-to-end encryption:

  • Files are encrypted on your device before upload using TweetNaCl secretbox (XSalsa20-Poly1305)
  • Each file is split into encrypted chunks
  • Encryption keys are derived from your credentials and stored only on your device
  • StealthLynk LLC cannot decrypt your data

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4.1 Data Integrity

PhotoLynk does not modify, alter, or change any metadata (EXIF data, creation date, location information, camera settings, etc.) of your photos or videos. Your files are stored exactly as they exist on your device, preserving all original properties and metadata intact.

5. Third-Party Services

Subscription payments are signed by your Solana wallet and settled directly on the Solana blockchain — no third-party payment processor handles your funds. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

6. Data Retention

For Local and Remote modes, data retention is entirely under your control. For StealthCloud mode, encrypted data is retained while your subscription is active, plus a grace period after expiration — 10 days for paid plans and earned entitlements, 3 days for free trials. Once the grace period ends, cloud uploads and access pause. Your backed-up data is then kept on our servers for a limited retention window — 30 days if you have ever held a paid plan, 10 days otherwise — after which it is permanently deleted. If you renew at any point before the window closes, your account and all backed-up data are restored immediately. You can delete your account at any time in the app or via the Account Deletion page; upon account deletion, all associated encrypted data is permanently deleted. NFT certificates on the Solana blockchain are not affected.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. This page uses no cookies or trackers.
