Account Deletion

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

Delete your StealthLynk account and all associated data permanently. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

1. What Gets Deleted

  • Account — Your email or wallet-derived sign-in identity and authentication credentials
  • Photos & Videos — All encrypted backups on StealthCloud
  • Devices — All device associations, sessions, and sync history
  • Ecosystem installs — Every Blipp and PaceSeeker install linked to your account or wallet, including their trials, subscriptions, and claims

What is never deleted: NFTs and certificates minted on Solana remain on the blockchain permanently. Payment records may be retained where required by law (tax, accounting) — they contain transaction references only.

2. Delete Your Account — Self-Service

The fastest way is the account deletion page — enter your account email (or wallet-derived address) and password:

Delete My Account Now

Deletion completes immediately: your account, devices, encrypted backups, and every linked Blipp/PaceSeeker install are removed in one step.

3. Prefer Email?

Email support@stealthlynk.io with subject Account Deletion Request from your account email address. Never send your password by email — we will verify your identity another way and process the request within 30 days.

4. Wallet-Only Accounts

If you signed up with a Solana wallet and never set a real email, use the wallet address shown in the app (Settings → account) when contacting support — your account identity is derived from it.
